Water damage restoration in Lavon, TX starts with an unusual local detail: the water does not come from the city. The City of Lavon says it provides sewer service only in certain areas and that water is supplied by Bear Creek Special Utility District. That affects who you call for a main break and who is responsible for a backup, and our 24/7 line works around either answer.

Who serves your water and sewer in Lavon

Bear Creek Special Utility District lists its office at 16881 CR 541 in Lavon and its phone as 972-843-2101. For a leak before the meter, a main break or a pressure problem, that is the call. The City of Lavon states it supplies sewer in certain areas, with City Hall at 120 School Road and the phone 972-843-4220.

If you live outside the city sewer area, your system may be private, and a backup or overflow may be your responsibility rather than the city's. A restoration crew handles what is inside your home, and a plumber or septic specialist handles the system outside.

Flood rules and Lavon Lake

The City of Lavon's flood ordinance states that flood hazard areas of the city are subject to periodic inundation, and it adopts the Flood Insurance Study for Collin County dated June 2, 2009. The city administrator serves as floodplain administrator.

Lavon Lake, operated by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers for flood control and water supply, is a short distance away near Wylie. Local creeks and low ground can still flood. Standard homeowner policies exclude floods, according to the Texas Department of Insurance, so check the FEMA map for your address.

When water is spreading in Lavon

  • Water is running inside the house. Close the main valve, which may be at the street meter or inside near the water heater.
  • Storm water is entering from a low yard. Move to higher rooms and call the electric utility or 911 for any electrical risk.
  • A septic or sewer line is overflowing. Keep people and pets out of the area.
  • A ceiling is stained and dripping. Place a bucket and keep clear of the sag.
  • It is after dark and you are unsure. The emergency line takes calls at any hour.
