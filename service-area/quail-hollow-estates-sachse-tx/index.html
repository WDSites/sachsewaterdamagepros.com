Who owns which pipe

The City of Sachse maintains water mains and service lines to the meter and sewer mains and laterals to the property line. Past that point, the lines are the homeowner's. That split decides who fixes the pipe, while the cleanup inside the house is a separate job.

Safety steps for a backup

  • Stop using water. Every flush or wash sends more into the lowest drain.
  • Keep people and pets out of the wet area.
  • Stay away from outlets and cords near the water. Call 911 or the electric utility for any electrical risk.
  • Wear boots and gloves if you must cross the area, and wash thoroughly.
  • Call a plumber or the city for the line and a professional for the cleanup.
  • Photograph the area before cleanup begins.

How the cleanup works

Standing water is extracted with equipment made for the job, then porous materials that touched sewage, such as carpet pad and drywall, are normally removed. Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated, and the structure is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers until readings level off. The reference for the work is the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard.

Clean supply water is a different case. A broken line or a failed hose may allow more material to be dried in place.

Insurance

The Texas Department of Insurance explains that flood needs a separate policy and that sudden and accidental water damage is generally covered. Sewer backup is often an endorsement, so ask your agent whether yours is on the policy. We cannot promise coverage.
