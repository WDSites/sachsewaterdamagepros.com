First-hour safety steps

  • Close the main water valve if you can reach it without standing in water.
  • Stay out of rooms where water touches outlets or cords. Call 911 or the electric utility for any electrical risk.
  • Keep children and pets away from sewage or dirty water.
  • Lift small items and furniture legs off wet floors only if the area is safe.
  • Photograph the damage and any failed part before cleanup.
  • Call a restoration professional for extensive or contaminated water.

How extraction and drying work

A technician maps moisture with meters and a thermal camera, because water travels under cabinets and into wall bases. Standing water is extracted first, then air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure until readings level off. The reference for the work is the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard.

Wet carpet pad and swollen cabinet bases are often removed, while many hard surfaces can be dried in place. Sewage or other contaminated water changes the plan, and household fans are not enough for it.

Insurance and documentation

The Texas Department of Insurance explains that sudden and accidental water damage is generally covered and gradual leaks are not. We cannot say what your policy covers or promise a claim result. Photos, moisture readings and a written scope help describe what happened.
