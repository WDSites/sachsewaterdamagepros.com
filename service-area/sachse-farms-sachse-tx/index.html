What to do when a ceiling stains

  • Place a bucket under the lowest drip and move belongings.
  • Stay clear of a sagging ceiling. Water and debris can drop at once.
  • Keep power off near wet fixtures. Call 911 or the electric utility for any electrical risk.
  • Cover roof openings only when it is safe. Do not climb a wet roof.
  • Photograph the stain and note the date it appeared.
  • Call a roofer for the leak and a restoration professional for the water.

From wet to repair-ready

A technician maps moisture above and below the ceiling and checks insulation, since it holds water against the board. The EPA says wet fiberglass and cellulose insulation should be discarded and replaced. Wallboard can be dried in place if it has not swollen and the seams are intact.

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the cavity. Once readings are dry, the wall is ready for new board, texture and paint.

Insurance notes

The Texas Department of Insurance explains that sudden and accidental water damage is generally covered and gradual leaks are not. A roof leak can fall on either side. We cannot promise coverage, so keep photos and read your policy.
