Why this page focuses on air conditioning

Air conditioners in this region run hard. The National Weather Service 1991 to 2020 normals for DFW put the July average high at 95.6 degrees and August at 95.8. Every cooling hour makes condensate that has to drain, and a blocked line sends it into an attic, a closet or a ceiling.

If you see water near the unit

  • Switch the system off at the thermostat. Running it makes more water.
  • Turn off the breaker only if the panel is dry and easy to reach.
  • Stay out of wet or very hot attics.
  • Place a bucket under a drip and move belongings off the floor.
  • Photograph the unit, the pan and the stain.
  • Call an HVAC contractor for the unit and a restoration professional for the water.

How drying works after an AC leak

Moisture meters and a thermal camera show where the water went. The EPA says wet fiberglass and cellulose insulation should be discarded and replaced, and the cavity should be ventilated. Ceiling board that has swollen is cut out in straight lines so repair is simple.

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the rest. Readings are checked on each visit until they level off. The reference for the work is the ANSI/IICRC S500 water damage standard.

Insurance

The Texas Department of Insurance explains that sudden and accidental water damage is generally covered and gradual leaks are not. A single overflow may read as sudden, and a leak that ran all summer may not. We cannot promise coverage. Keep service records and photos.
