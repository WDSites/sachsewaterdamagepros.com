What this service is

Sachse Water Damage Pros runs a 24/7 emergency line for homeowners in Sachse, Garland, Wylie, Rowlett and Lavon. You call, describe what happened, and dispatch sends a crew. The business address is 6406 S State Highway 78, Sachse, TX 75048.

We cover water extraction, water mitigation, cleanup, repair, flood restoration and mold. The services page explains each one.

Standards we point to

Two published standards guide the trade. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers water damage restoration, and ANSI/IICRC S520 covers mold remediation. We name them so you can ask any company whether its crews work to them. We do not claim a firm certification on this site.

What you can expect

  • A 24/7 line. Calls are answered at any hour.
  • Scope and price explained first. A technician measures and explains before work starts.
  • Documentation. Photos, moisture readings and a written scope you can share with your insurer.
  • Honest limits. We say when a problem belongs to a plumber, roofer or foundation specialist.

How a call becomes a job

You call and describe the water. Dispatch asks where it started and what it has reached, then sends a crew. On site, the technician finds the source, measures the damage and explains the scope and price before work starts. Extraction and drying come first, with moisture readings recorded as the work goes on.

Cleanup and rebuilding follow once the structure is dry. You can read each stage on the water mitigation, water damage cleanup and water damage repair pages. Mold work has its own page at mold remediation.

Insurance and documentation

Insurance rules come from your policy and the Texas Department of Insurance. The department says sudden and accidental water damage is generally covered, gradual leaks generally are not, and flood needs a separate policy. We keep photos, readings and a written scope that you can send to your insurer. We do not decide coverage and we cannot promise a claim will be paid.
