Privacy Policy

How Sachse Water Damage Pros handles information when you visit this site or call our line.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, address and a description of the damage so dispatch can send a crew. We use that information to respond to your request.

What this website collects

This site has no forms and does not ask you to type personal information. Our hosting provider and browser may record basic visit data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location for security and performance.

How information is used

We use call details to schedule and perform service, to explain scope and price and to answer questions about a job. We do not use the site to collect payment details.

Third parties

The site embeds Google Maps and loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when pages load. Those services have their own policies.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete call information we hold about you by calling (972) 945-2885 or emailing contact@sachsewaterdamagepros.com. Contact us with any privacy question at the same address.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is the one on this page.
